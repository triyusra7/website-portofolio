Privacy Policy

Hermes Jarvis, a personal automation tool by Tri Anugerah Yusra. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

What this app is

Hermes Jarvis is a private automation tool used only by its owner, Tri Anugerah Yusra, to manage his own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the app may access the owner's own Gmail (to send, read, and label email), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Contacts. Access is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for, such as sending business emails he has approved and checking replies to them.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and any data retrieved are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to a separate server operated by this app.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. It is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
